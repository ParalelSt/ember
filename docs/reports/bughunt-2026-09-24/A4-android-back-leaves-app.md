# A4. The Back button closed the Android app instead of going back
**What you'd notice:** in the Android app, pressing Back (or swiping back from the edge) with the full-screen player or search open, or on any page other than the first, closed the app instead of closing the sheet or going to the previous page.
**Why it happened:** the web app is ready for Back: the player and search sheets add a history step when they open and close when it is undone, and every page is a step. But the Android shell never passed Back to the page, so Android did its default and closed the app.
**What changed:** Back now steps back inside the page: it closes the open sheet, or goes to the previous page. On the first page it sends the app to the background, like Home, instead of closing it, so the music keeps its screen and opening the app again is instant. No web code changed. Files: `apps/mobile/android/.../BackButton.kt` (new), `MainActivity.java`.
**Compare:** before = `0251128`, after = `9919552`.
- Test: `cd apps/mobile/android && ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest --tests 'app.ember.music.BackButtonTest'` (JDK 21; a real activity and WebView under Robolectric). Before (no Back handling, as `MainActivity` had): 3 of 3 fail.
  - `the app was closed` (all three: Back with a page to go back to, on the first page, and before the page has loaded)
  - After: 3 of 3 pass, all 125 Kotlin tests pass, `assembleDebug` builds.
- Try it yourself (needs a new APK): install the new debug build. Play a song and tap the mini player to open the full-screen player; press Back: the player closes and you are on the same page. Open Search and press Back: search closes. Open a playlist from Home and press Back: you are on Home again. Press Back on Home: the app goes to the background and the music keeps playing; open it again from recent apps and it is right where you left it.
**Risk:** low. It only changes what Back does; if the page cannot go back, the app leaves as before (to the background instead of closing).
